Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Aisthetic ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Account data: When you register, we collect your name and email address.
  • Photos you upload: Images you submit for transformation are processed by our AI and then deleted from our servers. We do not store your photos permanently.
  • Usage data: We collect anonymous usage statistics (number of generations, styles used) to improve the app. This data is never linked to your identity.
  • Device token: We store your FCM push notification token to deliver in-app notifications. You can disable this in iOS Settings.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Aisthetic service
  • To send you relevant push notifications (you can opt out anytime)
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To detect and prevent abuse

Third-Party Services

Aisthetic uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, and push notifications
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management
  • Google AdMob — advertising (Free plan only)

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen in the app.

Children's Privacy

Aisthetic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at aistheticsupport@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy
